Hasnat Corporation | Decades of Craftsmanship, Global Reach.

Hasnat Corporation is a dedicated apparel manufacturing company focused on producing premium-quality garments for brands, businesses, teams, and global buyers. Our approach is built around a simple principle: combining skilled craftsmanship, modern manufacturing techniques, and consistent quality to create apparel that meets the demands of today’s competitive market.

From Fashion Wear and Sportswear to Fitness Wear, Boxing & MMA apparel, and custom uniforms, we provide end-to-end manufacturing solutions tailored to each client’s requirements. From the initial concept and product design to pattern making, fabric selection, production, finishing, quality control, and shipping, we carefully manage every stage of the manufacturing process.

Why Choose Us

At Hasnat Industry, we are a passionate and dedicated team of sportswear manufacturers delivering export-quality apparel and sports goods. We never compromise on quality and create every product with strict care and attention, ensuring comfort and reliability for long-term use. Therefore, choosing us means you are not only meeting your requirements but adding exceptional value to your brand. So, collaborate with HP Hasnat Industry where quality truly meets perfection.

Product Designing

If you have any vision for your sportswear design such as references or files share them with us. Our team will turn your ideas into reality with their professional creative touch. First, we will send you the digital design For approval, we will move to the next step.

Pattern Making

Once the design is approved, we create precise patterns for each apparel component. At HP Trading Japan, we ensure every measurement, cut, and shape is accurate. This step lays the foundation for producing high-quality and perfectly fitting sportswear.

Cutting & Printing

We select premium grade fabrics and cut them accurately according to the approved patterns. At this stage, we incorporate your branding, logo, and design through professional printing techniques. Our team ensures that your sportswear is durable & stylish.

Embroidery

Next, we enhance the appearance and performance of your custom sportswear through embroidery, heat transfer, or other detailing methods. This adds texture, identity, and a premium look to each piece, ensuring your apparel quality and design.

Production

During production, all components come together. Our experienced craftsmen stitch, assemble, and finish each garment with care. Every detail is inspected to ensure long-lasting performance, comfort, and reliability before moving to the final phase.

Shipping

Before shipping, each product is thoroughly checked under strict supervision to avoid any issues. The HP Trading Japan team ensures safe, clean, and secure packaging with a commitment to fast delivery so your order arrives at your doorstep on time.
